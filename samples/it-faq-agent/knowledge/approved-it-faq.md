# Northstar Lab IT FAQ

Fictional training organization. Approved for this demo only, not real workplace policy.
Document version: 1.0. Owner: Northstar Lab training team. Effective: 2026-09-05.

## FAQ-01: When is the lab help desk open?

The fictional lab help desk is open Monday through Friday, 09:00-17:00 UTC.
It is closed on Saturday and Sunday. This FAQ does not define holiday hours,
response times, resolution targets, or any emergency service.
The assistant cannot contact the help desk.

## FAQ-02: How do I request software?

For this exercise, choose an application in the fictional Lab Software Catalog
and send its name and business purpose through your organization's existing
approved support channel. Manager approval is required before a paid license
can be assigned. Approval does not guarantee availability.
This demo provides no live catalog, address, purchase action, or installation tool.

## FAQ-03: What should I try if the lab VPN will not connect?

Check that your internet connection works. Close and reopen the VPN application,
then try connecting once more. If it still fails, use your organization's
existing approved support channel and describe the symptom without secrets.
Do not disable security software, change firewall rules, or share sign-in codes.
The assistant cannot inspect your device or determine whether there is an outage.

## FAQ-04: Can the assistant reset my password or MFA?

No. Use your organization's existing approved account recovery process.
Never send a password, verification code, recovery code, or MFA approval to
this assistant. The demo has no recovery link and cannot change account access.

## FAQ-05: Can I get a loaner laptop?

In this fictional lab, loaner laptops are for temporary use while an assigned
laptop is being repaired. Availability must be checked by the lab help desk.
No duration, reservation, delivery date, or approval is guaranteed by this FAQ.
The assistant cannot reserve or order a device.
